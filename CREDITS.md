## Current bundled assets

The local environment could not complete HTTPS downloads from OpenGameArt during implementation, so this build uses remote-open-art loading where available plus procedural 8-bit jungle/base fallback rendering and WebAudio-generated sound effects. The current fallback art is tuned toward a late-80s Japanese run-and-gun look: crisp pixels, high-contrast jungle layers, military sprites, hard-edged weapons, base blocks, bullets, pickups, and a mechanical boss. It remains original work and does not bundle Contra copyrighted art, names, logos, or music.
